# Chatbots and Customer Satisfaction  
Maria Salop – Breda University of Applied Sciences  

## Overview  
This project investigates the impact of chatbots on **customer trust**, **satisfaction**, and **business benefits**. With the growing role of AI-driven interactions, the research focuses on how chatbot **empathy, reliability, and interaction quality** affect customer experiences, particularly in sensitive contexts.  

The work was developed as part of the Applied Data Science & AI program and combines **quantitative research**, **EDA**, and **policy recommendations**.  

---

## Individual Research Paper  
**Title:** Analyzing the Influence of Empathetic Language in Chatbots on Customer Trust and Comfort During Sensitive Inquiries  

This paper explores how empathetic communication in chatbots influences user trust and comfort. Survey data was analyzed with statistical methods including **correlation analysis**, **linear regression**, and **hypothesis testing**.  

Key findings:  
- Empathy in chatbot responses has a **statistically significant positive effect** on customer comfort and trust (r = 0.55, p < 0.05).  
- Visualizations such as heatmaps, scatterplots, and KDE plots support the relationship between empathy and customer trust.  
- Results suggest that empathetic chatbot design enhances **user experience**, **trust**, and **satisfaction** in sensitive situations.

## Policy Paper  
**Topic:** Ethical AI Integration in Customer Service Chatbots  

The policy paper provides actionable strategies for implementing chatbots in SMEs with a focus on **trust**, **empathy**, and **data protection**. It outlines:  
- Risks such as privacy, reliability, and system vulnerabilities.  
- Opportunities for improving customer engagement and loyalty.  
- Recommendations for responsible chatbot adoption aligned with GDPR and ethical AI principles.
